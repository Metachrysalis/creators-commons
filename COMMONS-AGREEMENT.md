# Creators Commons Agreement

## Creator Rights

A Creator may:

* exercise all Players Commons rights;
* Adapt eligible Commons Material;
* contribute original works;
* register adaptations;
* choose a Creator Compensation Weight for contributed works;
* participate in applicable Creator Participation Pools; and
* participate in Creators Commons governance.

## Ownership

Creators retain copyright in their contributions unless they expressly transfer it.

Contribution to the Commons is a license, not an assignment of ownership.

## Standing Commons Permission

By registering a Work, the Creator grants eligible Commons members the rights stated in the Players and Creators Commons licenses.

The Creator also agrees that an organization holding valid Sustaining Organization authorization may exercise the organizational rights applicable to that Work, subject to:

* the Commons licenses;
* the Work's registered compensation status;
* Commons-Alike;
* Contribute-Back;
* attribution; and
* applicable organizational terms.

This permission is granted directly by the Creator through registration of the Work.

## Creator Compensation

A Creator may designate a registered Work as:

**Gift** — no direct Creator Participation payment requested; or

**Participating** — entitled to participate according to its registered Commercial Weight.

Compensation settings operate prospectively.

## Accuracy

A Creator must not knowingly contribute material they lack authority to license.

Third-party elements should be identified where reasonably practicable.

## Attribution

Creators should respect requested attribution, pseudonymity, and anonymity.

## Adaptations

Creators should preserve meaningful lineage between an Adaptation and the works from which it derives.

## No Enclosure

A Creator may commercially benefit from their own contribution but may not use Commons membership to extinguish the Commons rights already attached to contributed material.
