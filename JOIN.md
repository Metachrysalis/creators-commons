# Joining the Creators Commons

## Eligibility

Applicants must first qualify for Players Commons participation or simultaneously accept the Players Commons responsibilities.

## Application

Applicants provide:

* preferred public name or pseudonym;
* verifiable identifier;
* acceptance of the Creators Commons License;
* acceptance of the Creators Commons Agreement; and
* a brief statement of interest in contributing to the Commons.

No contribution is required merely to apply unless governance later establishes such a requirement.

## Approval

Membership begins upon entry in `MEMBERS.md` or another effective date recorded by the Commons.

## Membership Is Not Purchased

There is no required Creator membership fee.
