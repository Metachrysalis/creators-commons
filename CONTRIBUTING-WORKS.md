# Contributing Work to the Creators Commons

## 1. Create a Work Record

Every contributed work receives a stable Work ID.

Example:

`MC-WORK-000123`

## 2. Provide the Work

The work may be:

* stored directly in a Commons repository;
* linked to an external repository;
* stored in an approved media archive; or
* identified by another stable location.

## 3. Register Attribution

Specify:

* title;
* creator or creators;
* requested attribution;
* version;
* source location;
* description;
* date contributed; and
* relevant third-party material.

## 4. Choose Compensation Status

Choose:

### Gift

No direct Creator Participation payment is required for compensated bundles using the Work.

or:

### Participating

Choose a Commercial Weight from 1 through 10.

The weight represents the creator's chosen relative economic weight for distribution from Creator Participation Pools.

It is not a fixed dollar price.

## 5. Multiple Creators

Co-creators specify a payout split totalling 100%.

Example:

* Bog Witch — 60%
* River Otter — 40%

## 6. Adapted Works

An Adaptation should identify its upstream Work IDs.

The adaptation may define a lineage allocation showing how payments attributable to that Adaptation should be distributed between the new contribution and upstream contributions.

Lineage allocations must total 100%.

## 7. Acceptance

A work enters the Commons when its Work Record is accepted and merged into the registry.

The Git commit provides a durable record of when the contribution became registered.

## 8. Changing Compensation Weight

A creator may change the weight prospectively.

Changes do not alter compensation already earned.

Existing fixed-term bundle agreements may continue using the previous weight for their stated term, up to a maximum period established by the Commons.
