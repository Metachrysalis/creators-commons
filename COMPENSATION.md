# Creator Participation and Compensation

## 1. Purpose

Commons participation should enable creative recombination without treating contributors as free raw material for other people's compensated activity.

When compensation is generated substantially through a bundle incorporating registered Commons works, participating creators should share in that value.

## 2. Compensated Bundle

A **Compensated Bundle** is a workshop, event, product, publication, performance, program, service, training, experience, package, or other offering for which compensation is received and which substantively incorporates one or more registered Commons works.

A Bundle may be an Adaptation, a collection of independent works, or another combination.

## 3. Creator Participation Pool

Unless an applicable agreement specifies another rate, the draft default Creator Participation Pool is:

**20% of Attributable Bundle Revenue.**

This percentage is provisional during version 0.1.

## 4. Attributable Bundle Revenue

Attributable Bundle Revenue means compensation directly attributable to the compensated offering, excluding:

* sales taxes;
* refunds;
* separately itemized pass-through travel;
* separately itemized venue expenses;
* separately itemized catering;
* and other third-party pass-through expenses approved by policy.

Ordinary wages, facilitator compensation, profit, and internal overhead are not automatically treated as pass-through deductions.

## 5. Commercial Weight

A participating Work has a creator-selected Commercial Weight from 1 through 10.

A Gift Work has weight 0.

The Pool is divided proportionally according to the weights of participating Works included in the Bundle.

## 6. Example

A workshop includes:

* Bog Witch Song — weight 3
* Bog Witch Script — weight 5
* Forest Map — weight 4
* Facilitation Cards — weight 8

Total weight: 20.

Bog Witch's works represent 8 of the 20 units.

Bog Witch therefore receives 40% of the Creator Participation Pool, subject to any registered co-creator or lineage splits.

## 7. Co-Creators

The payment attributed to a Work is divided using that Work's registered creator split.

## 8. Adaptation Lineage

Where a registered Adaptation incorporates protected contributions from upstream registered Works, its Work Record may define a lineage split.

Example:

`MC-WORK-000240`

* current adapter: 60%
* `MC-WORK-000123`: 25%
* `MC-WORK-000075`: 15%

The adaptation's share of a Creator Participation Pool is divided accordingly.

This avoids requiring every downstream Bundle to reconstruct copyright history manually.

## 9. Manifest

Every Compensated Bundle must maintain a Bundle Manifest identifying participating Works.

The manifest may remain confidential where necessary, but sufficient records must exist to audit Creator Participation.

## 10. Reporting

Creator Participation amounts should be calculated and reported on a regular schedule.

The eventual 1.0 policy should specify:

* reporting frequency;
* minimum payout threshold;
* payment methods;
* currency conversion;
* abandoned/unclaimed payments;
* administrative deductions; and
* dispute procedures.

## 11. Direct Negotiation

A Creator and Bundle operator may agree to different compensation for a particular use.

Such an agreement should be recorded in writing.

It does not alter the Work's default Commons status for unrelated uses.

## 12. No Compensation Where None Exists

A genuinely uncompensated community activity does not create a monetary Creator Participation Pool merely because Commons works are used.

Organizational licensing obligations may still apply independently.
