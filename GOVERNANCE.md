# Creators Commons Governance

## 1. Relationship to Metachrysalis Governance

The Creators Commons operates under the governance framework established in:

https://github.com/Metachrysalis/commons/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md

Creators Commons members are **Participating Members**.

Creators Commons membership does not by itself confer Voting Membership.

Formal governance authority is held by the Voting Members recorded in:

https://github.com/Metachrysalis/commons/blob/main/VOTING-MEMBERS.md

## 2. Creator Participation

Creators are encouraged to participate actively in development and stewardship of the Creators Commons.

Creators may:

* propose Commons policy;
* participate in discussions and consultations;
* recommend changes to compensation rules;
* advise on contribution standards;
* raise attribution or lineage concerns;
* participate in working groups;
* review proposed systems and practices; and
* contribute to the creative culture and evolution of the Commons.

These rights provide meaningful opportunities to influence the Commons without automatically conferring a formal vote.

## 3. Formal Governance

Formal governance includes decisions concerning:

* Creators Commons membership;
* contribution standards;
* work-registration policy;
* Creator Participation rules;
* compensation policy;
* disputes concerning lineage or attribution;
* delegation of administrative functions; and
* amendments to Creators Commons policy.

Formal decisions are made under the overarching Metachrysalis governance framework.

## 4. Delegation

Routine administration, work registration, review, facilitation, and other defined responsibilities may be delegated.

Delegation does not confer Voting Membership.

## 5. Organizational Licensing

Organizational authorization is governed through the broader Metachrysalis Commons framework.

Creator membership alone does not authorize an organization.

## 6. Compensation Changes

Changes to compensation formulas should:

* operate prospectively;
* provide reasonable notice where practical; and
* not erase compensation already earned under earlier terms.

Creators should be consulted on material changes to Creator Participation policy where practical.

## 7. Disputes

Good-faith disputes concerning work ownership, attribution, lineage, or compensation may result in temporary suspension of payments associated with the disputed work while the matter is reviewed.

Undisputed payments should continue where practical.

A review or recommendation may be delegated, but final governance authority remains governed by the Metachrysalis Commons governance framework.

## 8. Changes

Changes to Creators Commons governance operate prospectively.

Git history provides the public version record.
