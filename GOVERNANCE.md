# Creators Commons Governance

## Responsibilities

Creators Commons governance oversees:

* membership;
* contribution standards;
* work registration;
* compensation policy;
* disputes concerning lineage or attribution;
* Creator Participation rules;
* adaptations of Commons policy; and
* delegation of administrative functions.

## Consent and Delegation

The Commons should ordinarily seek consent and may delegate routine administration to designated stewards or circles.

## Licensing Decisions

Organizational authorization is governed through the broader Metachrysalis Commons framework.

Creator membership alone does not authorize an organization.

## Compensation Changes

Changes to compensation formulas should operate prospectively and provide reasonable notice.

## Disputes

Good-faith disputes concerning work ownership, attribution, lineage, or compensation may result in temporary suspension of payments associated with the disputed work while the matter is reviewed.

Undisputed payments should continue where practical.
